
## 12. Falsification outcomes

A valid external test must allow failure.


## 13. Interpretation rule

A successful external comparison would increase evidence that the closed candidate theory describes nature. It would not retroactively create the mathematical theory — that internal package already exists.

A failed external comparison would falsify or constrain the candidate’s physical applicability. It would likewise not erase exact finite mathematical identities that remain true within the model.

That separation between **internal closure** and **empirical truth** is the canonical status policy of this repository.
