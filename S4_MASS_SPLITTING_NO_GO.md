## 1. What the 8.43% actually measures

\[

\[

\[

\[

\[
+\frac35\quad\text{on the 2D }E\text{ sector},

\]

The 8.43% is therefore a **two-sector E-versus-T2 metric stiffness anisotropy**.

It is not a three-eigenvalue flavor spectrum.

## 2. Schur's-lemma obstruction to three masses

Suppose, only as a test, that three generations of a matter species transformed as one irreducible tetrahedral triplet `T2`.

An unbroken `S4`-invariant mass operator `M` must obey

\[
D_{T_2}(g)M D_{T_2}(g)^{-1}=M
\qquad\forall g\in S_4.
\]

Because `T2` is irreducible, Schur's lemma gives

\[
\boxed{M=mI_3}.
\]

Therefore all three states are degenerate.

The geometric anisotropy does not evade this result.  Restricted to `T2`,

\[

=-\frac25 I_3.

Hence a direct coupling such as

\[

So the statement


is ruled out as a direct symmetry-preserving identification.
