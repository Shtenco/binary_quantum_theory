# Graph-changing quantum HDA target

Status: **conceptual correction and new falsification target**.

## 1. Why fixed-K5 leakage is not an HDA anomaly by itself

The previous finite diagnostics often projected

$$
[H_i,H_j]|\psi\rangle
$$

back to one fixed all-`j=1/2` K5 sector and called the orthogonal component a
`graph-changing anomaly`.  That terminology is too strong for a Thiemann/QSD-
type canonical Hamiltonian.

In QSD the Wheeler--DeWitt/Hamiltonian constraint is graph-changing: its action
on spin-network states creates, annihilates and reroutes angular-momentum
quanta carried by graph edges.  QSD III further emphasizes that the
Wheeler--DeWitt constraint mixes diffeomorphism superselection sectors and
formulates the non-anomalous algebra through dual constraint operators and the
diffeomorphism-invariant/group-averaged Hilbert space.

Primary references:

- T. Thiemann, *Quantum Spin Dynamics (QSD)*, arXiv:gr-qc/9606089.
- T. Thiemann, *QSD III: Quantum Constraint Algebra and Physical Scalar Product
  in Quantum General Relativity*, arXiv:gr-qc/9705017.

Therefore

$$
(1-P_{fixed})[H_i,H_j]|\psi\rangle\ne0
$$

is a useful **graph/spin-change diagnostic**, but it is not by itself a quantum
HDA anomaly.
