# Nonlinear TT response (finite edge stretch)

Under kinetic convention \(S_{\mathrm{kin}}=-S_{\mathrm{Regge}}\):

## Scan

Along a continuum TT polarization embedded in edge elongations,

\[
\Delta S_{\mathrm{kin}}(\varepsilon) \approx a_2\varepsilon^2 + a_3\varepsilon^3 + a_4\varepsilon^4.
\]

| coefficient | typical |
|---|--:|
| \(a_2\) | \(+0.08\text{–}0.12\) (>0) |
| \(|a_3|/|a_2|\) | \(\sim 10^{-3}\text{–}10^{-1}\) |
| \(a_4\) | \(O(10^{-4})\) |

## Meaning

- Quadratic positivity extends to a **finite** TT stretch (not only Hessian).
- Odd terms are small near the flat background (approximate evenness).
- This is a **local nonlinear probe**, not a blocked effective action.
