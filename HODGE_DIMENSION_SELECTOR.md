# Hodge-duality dimension selector

Status: **new structural hypothesis / falsification gate; not a derivation of dimension until the duality map is generated by a frozen microscopic rule**.

## Motivation

The binary-to-geometry problem should not be solved by choosing a 4D lattice, a target valence, a rank-4 tensor, eight spatial neighbours, sixteen descendants per block, or any other input that already contains the desired dimension.

CIMFIG already has two local geometric objects before any metric is assumed:

1. oriented frame/connection data on links;
2. gauge-invariant curvature information in loop/diamond holonomies.

These naturally become differential forms of different degree in a smooth phase.  This suggests a dimension selector based on **closure of the microscopic observable algebra under Hodge duality**, rather than on a chosen coordination number.

## Proposition A — spacetime 2-form self-duality singles out four dimensions

On an oriented `d`-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian continuum, the Hodge star maps

\[
*:\Lambda^p\longrightarrow\Lambda^{d-p}.
\]

Curvature of a connection is a 2-form.  If the continuum limit of the microscopic loop/holonomy sector is required to admit a local invertible duality operation which maps the curvature 2-form back into the **same 2-form observable sector**, then

\[
d-2=2,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{d=4}.
\]

This statement is purely about form degree.  It does not assume a four-dimensional coordinate chart.

For `p=2,d=4`, the square of the Hodge operator is signature dependent: in Euclidean signature the 2-form sector can be split into real self-dual and anti-self-dual sectors, while in Lorentzian signature the corresponding eigenvalues are complex.  A binary microscopic label could at most label these two **duality sectors**; it must not be identified with the full curvature tensor or with an entire graviton state.

## Proposition B — canonical edge/face duality singles out three spatial dimensions

If the microscopic graph represents one spatial slice and causal rewrite depth represents time, use spatial dimension `D` instead.  The spatial Hodge star gives

\[
*_\Sigma:C^1\longrightarrow C^{D-1},
\qquad
*_\Sigma:C^2\longrightarrow C^{D-2}.
\]

The existing microscopic alphabet is naturally connection-like on edges (`1`-cochains) and curvature-like on elementary loops/faces (`2`-cochains).  If one frozen local dynamics exchanges these sectors as dual descriptions of the same propagating frame information, closure requires

\[
D-1=2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
D-2=1,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{D=3}.
\]

If the independent dynamical exponent also approaches

\[
z\to1,
\]

then the emergent causal theory has a `3+1` relativistic scaling structure.

## Spectral-dimension consistency relation

For a continuum kinetic operator with anisotropic scaling

\[
K(\omega,k)\sim \omega^2+|k|^{2z}
\]

in `D` spatial dimensions, the heat return probability scales as

\[
P(\sigma)
\propto
\int d\omega\,d^Dk\,
\exp[-\sigma(\omega^2+|k|^{2z})]
\propto
\sigma^{-\frac12-\frac{D}{2z}}.
\]

Therefore the full history/spacetime spectral dimension is

\[
\boxed{d_s^{\rm history}=1+\frac{d_s^{\rm slice}}{z}}
\]

when the spatial spectral dimension is the relevant `D` in the scaling window.

A canonical `3+1` limit must therefore satisfy three independently measured statements:

\[
d_s^{\rm slice}\to3,
\qquad
z\to1,
\qquad
 d_s^{\rm history}\to4,
\]

plus the consistency defect

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{3+1}
=
\left|
 d_s^{\rm history}
-\left(1+\frac{d_s^{\rm slice}}{z}\right)
\right|
\to0.
}
\]

This prevents counting the causal time direction twice.  If the microscopic object being diffused on is already the full causal history complex, one should target `d_s -> 4` directly and **not** add another time dimension.

## Operational lattice gate

A statement about Hodge duality is useful only if the duality map is not fitted after seeing the target dimension.

For each blocking scale `b`, construct from the frozen microscopic rule:

- a coarse 1-cochain space `C^1_b` for edge/frame observables;
- a coarse 2-cochain space `C^2_b` for elementary loop/holonomy observables;
- their Hodge/de Rham Laplacians `Delta_1(b)` and `Delta_2(b)`;
- a local map `J_b` induced by the microscopic update/duality rule, not by post-hoc spectral matching.

Then measure

\[
\epsilon_{\rm iso}(b)
=
\frac{\|J_b^\dagger J_b-I\|}{\|I\|},
\]

\[
\epsilon_{\rm int}(b)
=
\frac{\|J_b\Delta_1-\Delta_2J_b\|}
{\|\Delta_1\|+\|\Delta_2\|},
\]

and a locality defect `epsilon_loc(b)` measuring how far the image of a local cochain spreads in graph distance relative to the block scale.

For an edge/face Hodge closure candidate require

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon_{\rm iso},
\epsilon_{\rm int},
\epsilon_{\rm loc}
\to0
}
\]

in the **same** scaling window in which independent graph observables give `d_s^slice -> 3` and `z -> 1`.

A stronger test constructs maps in both directions and requires the composition to approach the signature-appropriate Hodge-square relation.

## Why this is not yet a proof of 4D

The argument is conditional on a new physical requirement: the microscopic frame/holonomy algebra must actually possess a local duality which survives coarse graining.  Merely observing edges and loops does not imply such a duality.

The hypothesis is rejected if:

1. no local scale-stable `J_b` is generated by the frozen rule;
2. a fitted `J_b` is required after looking at the spectra;
3. edge and loop sectors have different IR spectra with finite mismatch;
4. independent dimension estimators do not approach `3` on slices / `4` on the full history complex;
5. the result appears only for one blocking prescription.

Thus the proposal does not replace the dimension measurement.  It supplies a possible **reason** for why a connection-first microscopic theory could select `3+1` rather than a rule designer inserting the number four.
