# Scalar source / gauge ordering correction

Status: **senior physicalization correction; freezes a distinction required before interpreting any scalar kernel.**

## 1. The problem

The physicalization programme correctly requires the actual graph-changing constraint family before observables are called physical. However, one must not conflate two different operations:

1. projection/rigging onto the physical history sector;
2. construction of an off-shell/background-field metric effective action in which lapse and shift still enforce scalar constraints.

In canonical ADM language, lapse `N` and shift `N^i` are Lagrange multipliers. They are not ordinary propagating operators on the final physical Hilbert space.

Therefore the shortcut

```text
P_phys
-> treat lapse and shift as ordinary physical operators
-> source them like volume/shape
-> invert their correlator
```

is not a valid production definition of `Psi` or a dark scalar.

## 2. Two legal routes

### Route A — background-field / off-shell effective action

Use the physical projector/history construction to define the gravitational measure or boundary amplitude, but keep external/background metric data off shell:

\[
\boxed{
\{C_A\}
\to \mathbb M
\to \text{physical history weighting}
\to Z[g_{\mu\nu},J_{matter}]
\to W
\to \Gamma[g]
}
\]

before solving the scalar constraints.

In an ADM scalar decomposition, organize the Hessian in variables schematically

\[
\chi=(c,s),
\qquad
c=(\delta N,B),
\qquad
s=(\zeta,E)
\]

or in their discrete analogues.

Then

\[
\Gamma^{(2)}_{scalar}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
K_{cc}&K_{cs}\\
K_{sc}&K_{ss}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Only after the Ward/gauge structure is frozen may the non-propagating constraint variables be eliminated. On an invertible reduced constraint block,

\[
\boxed{
K_{red}=K_{ss}-K_{sc}K_{cc}^{-1}K_{cs}.
}
\]

Gauge null directions require the corresponding Dirac/BRST/gauge-quotient prescription rather than an arbitrary inverse.

### Route B — fully physical relational observables

If one works only after projection to the final physical Hilbert space, then source insertions must themselves be physical/gauge-invariant relational observables:

\[
\boxed{
\bar O_a
=Q_0^\dagger O_a Q_0,
\qquad
Q_0=P_0B(B^\dagger P_0B)^{-1/2}.
}
\]

The resulting connected correlators can describe physical scalar response, but `delta N` and longitudinal shift cannot be reintroduced afterward as independent physical operators.

A Newtonian/Bardeen response must then be reconstructed directly from gauge-invariant relational metric observables and the response to a conserved source.

## 3. What remains common to both routes

Both routes must use the same theory-specific history weighting and the same emergent metric normalization.

They must agree on gauge-invariant observables. In particular, after all reductions they should produce the same response for

- the Newtonian potential entering nonrelativistic dynamics;
- the Weyl potential entering lensing;
- any additional physical scalar pole.

This gives a future nontrivial equivalence test between an off-shell Schur/Dirac reduction and a purely relational physical-observable calculation.

## 5. How the collective j=1 volume can activate without changing the external q=2 boundary

The fixed all-`j=1/2` boundary has scalar absolute volume inside its local intertwiner qubit. This does **not** force the physical dressed boundary state to have trivial volume response.

Let

\[
Q_0=P_0B G_0^{-1/2},
\qquad
G_0=B^\dagger P_0B.
\]

Even if every column of `B` is initially all-`j=1/2`, the enlarged physical image `P_0B` can contain higher Peter-Weyl representations generated by the constraints.

Therefore the correct projected collective-volume operator is

\[
\boxed{
\bar V=Q_0^\dagger V_{collective}Q_0.
}
\]

A nontrivial physical volume susceptibility requires not merely a nonzero mean but a non-scalar projected operator, equivalently nonzero variance in the chosen physical state:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}_{phys}(\bar V)
=\langle\bar V^2\rangle-\langle\bar V\rangle^2>0.
}
\]

If

\[
\bar V=v_0 I
\]

on the physical boundary support, then the local volume source only shifts the source normalization and does not provide an independent scalar fluctuation channel.

Thus it is unnecessary to insert a hand-chosen `j=1` external boundary state merely to obtain a volume variable. The first production test should ask whether physical constraint dressing of the q=2 boundary itself activates the already-known collective `j=1` volume structure.
