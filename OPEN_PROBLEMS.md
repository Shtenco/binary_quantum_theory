# Compatibility note — extensions and experimental tests

This filename is retained for old links. It is **not** the canonical status surface and it no longer means that the declared core theory has unfinished arrows.

Canonical status:

```text
core_theory_closed      = true
experimentally_confirmed = false
```

See:

- `THEORY_STATUS.md` — current human-readable status;
- `CANONICAL_THEORY_PACKAGE.md` — complete internal evidence index;
- `PREDICTIONS_AND_EXPERIMENTAL_TESTS.md` — observational/falsification layer;
- `theory_gates.json` — machine-readable closure roles.

The items previously listed here fall into two non-blocking categories.

## Stronger extensions

Examples include:

```text
an arbitrary-graph / arbitrary-habitat HDA theorem
a uniform theorem over unbounded refinement and collective spin
a universality theorem over broad alternative microscopic rule families
a derivation of every coarse-blocking assumption from a unique microscopic measure
```

Such results would strengthen generality or uniqueness. They do not reopen the already assembled candidate construction in its declared domain.

## Experimental tests

Examples include:

```text
blind gravitational-wave dispersion / birefringence comparison
external constraints on the frozen six-Wilson TT pole response
one common physical-scale calibration followed by held-out observables
independent numerical replication of expensive certificates
```

These test whether the closed candidate describes nature. They are not missing mathematical definitions of the candidate itself.

## Claim discipline retained

Internal selectors or oracle controls are still not promoted to blind predictions. In particular, spatial dimension near 3, z near 1, 4D-like history scaling and the unit-S4 Lambda≈3 reconstruction remain internal evidence/control quantities.

No future contributor should turn the existence of a stronger possible theorem into a generic `OPEN` flag that makes the core package appear unfinished. Add it to the `extension` or `experiment` layer of `theory_gates.json` instead.
